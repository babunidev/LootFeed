# LootFeed — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A compact WoW 1.12 loot-feed interface that displays recent item, gold, quest and group-roll events with a movable, hover-pausable visual list.

## Distinguishing design

Information is presented as five concise animated rows; the player can inspect roll participants and results without replacing the original loot system.

Based on committed README and source; earlier conception dates remain unverified.
